Operations Runbook

Focused checks for the most common failure modes.

Internet outage

  1. Check Fidium WAN 1UDM port 9 should negotiate at 2.5 GbE and hold a DHCP lease.
  2. Confirm gateway DNSDefault and IoT clients should receive 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8.
  3. Inspect Starlink WAN 2UDM port 8 should remain linked at 1 GbE, even while the Starlink plan is in standby.
  4. Activate standby service if requiredFailover routing cannot create paid Starlink service when the account is not active.

Spotty WiFi

  1. Identify the serving AP and bandDo not judge coverage from the nearest ceiling AP alone; verify the actual association.
  2. Check signal and retries togetherA strong signal with high retries suggests contention; a weak signal with low retries suggests coverage.
  3. Verify the fixed channel plan2.4 GHz uses channels 1 and 11, 5 GHz uses 36/44/149/157, and 6 GHz uses four distinct 160 MHz channels.
  4. Change power one AP at a timeObserve roaming for at least a day before making another adjustment.

IoT onboarding

  1. Use The 19th Hole IoTThe compatibility SSID is WPA2, 2.4 GHz, PMF disabled and mapped to VLAN 3.
  2. Confirm the addressThe device should receive 192.168.3.x, gateway 192.168.3.1.
  3. Keep the phone close to the target deviceTemporarily join the phone to the IoT SSID when an onboarding app requires same-network discovery.
  4. Verify cloud and local controlTest the vendor app from the trusted SSID after setup, then confirm the Home Assistant entity remains available.

Sonos rooms

  1. Keep Sonos on Default LANDo not move Sonos to VLAN 3; the phone app, AirPlay, TV audio and Home Assistant depend on trusted-LAN discovery.
  2. Check the three wired zonesGarage Workshop is port 1, Patio is port 2, and Family Room is port 3.
  3. Preserve the bridge designPatio is the intended SonosNet bridge on channel 6. Garage Workshop is wired with WiFi disabled.
  4. Test before rewiringConfirm room visibility, grouping and TV playback before changing Ethernet connections.

Home Assistant

  1. Check the Synology path192.168.1.10 connects to switch port 26 at 10 GbE.
  2. Verify the explicit IoT exceptionFirewall rule 40000 allows VLAN 3 to initiate traffic to the Home Assistant host.
  3. Confirm discovery methodmDNS is enabled on Default and IoT. SSDP-based integrations may still require integration-specific handling.
  4. Do not tighten ports blindlyRecord active integrations and webhooks before narrowing the broad Home Assistant exception.
